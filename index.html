Panel CSV for MaxCut

Export selected rectangular panel bodies from Autodesk Fusion into a compatible MaxCut cutting list.

Review geometry, quantities, stock thickness and identities before exporting a CSV and its original-name mapping. Runs locally; no customer account or file upload.

Release status: pending Autodesk Marketplace review. No sales or customer download is currently offered through this page.

Supported workflow

  1. Select rectangular solid panel bodies in Fusion.
  2. Preview dimensions, quantity and the intended existing MaxCut material.
  3. Export panels.csv and name-map.json.
  4. Import into MaxCut and verify the assigned material in every row.

Important limitations

Windows only; plain solid cuboids; one material/thickness per batch. No holes, grooves, curves, grain inference, edging, nesting, automatic assembly traversal or automatic access to the MaxCut material library. MaxCut may report successful import even with an Unknown material; discard that job and correct the material before cutting. No comparative time-savings claims are made.
