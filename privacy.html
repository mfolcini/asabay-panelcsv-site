This policy covers the add-in and voluntarily submitted customer support communications.

Local Fusion data

The add-in measures user-selected bodies in the active Fusion design. It reads design and body names, rectangular-solid geometry, dimensions, volumes, axes, occurrence paths, identifiers and selection context. You supply quantities, an existing MaxCut material name and nominal thickness, and an export destination. It does not modify or save your design when exporting.

The add-in writes panels.csv (safe panel names, length, width, quantities, material and rotation) and name-map.json (exact original names, identities, geometry and provenance) into the export folder you choose. These files remain under your control.

Settings, logs and retention on your device

%APPDATA%\\PanelCsvMaxCut\\settings.json stores the last successfully used material name, nominal thickness and export parent. %APPDATA%\\PanelCsvMaxCut\\logs\\YYYYMMDD.jsonl stores local operational events, selected body/design names, file paths, identifiers, geometry, preview and validation values, error traces and export outcome. Some previews are logged even if export is later cancelled.

These diagnostic logs have no automatic expiry and accumulate until you remove them. Your exports and any local development-installation backups also remain until you remove them. Uninstalling the add-in does not delete these files. Stop the add-in and remove %APPDATA%\\PanelCsvMaxCut to delete its local settings and logs; remove export folders and product-specific backups separately where appropriate. Your next use may recreate files.

Exports in OneDrive or other synchronized locations can be copied by your chosen service; deleting local files may not remove backup, sync or MaxCut copies.

It does not automatically send geometry, export files, settings or logs to AsaBay LLC or third parties.
