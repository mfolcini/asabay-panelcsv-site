Support

For questions about the Panel CSV for MaxCut add-in, write to asabayllc+panelcsv@gmail.com. This routes to AsaBay LLC's administrative Gmail mailbox; it is not a separate managed account. Contact this address for product support.

When contacting support

Include your Fusion and MaxCut versions, the exact error and brief reproduction steps. Screenshots or redacted log excerpts are optional. Please do not send proprietary CAD files or unredacted customer documents unless we specifically agree on a secure transfer method.

Common problems

  • Export disabled: check that all selected bodies are plain cuboids, that the nominal material thickness matches and that you selected a new output folder.
  • Material shows Unknown in MaxCut: do not cut from this job. Discard it and verify the exact material name in the MaxCut library.
  • Panel names differ: the exporter uses unique safe labels. Refer to name-map.json for original names and occurrences.
  • Toolbar missing: verify that the add-in is running in Fusion's Scripts and Add-Ins and inspect the Design workspace Utilities / Add-Ins.
